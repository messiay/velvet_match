What is Velvet Match?

Not an app.
A real night out.

We're tired of swiping. You probably are too. Velvet Match is a curated, in-person matchmaking event where you show up, the vibe is right, and you actually meet people worth meeting.

Everyone fills in a personality profile before the night. We run a compatibility algorithm behind the scenes. When the clock hits midnight, every guest gets a personalised match notification — your name, their name, your score. What you do with it is up to you.

How It Works

Simple. Intentional. Unforgettable.

Four steps stand between you and the most interesting night of your year.

01

Fill Your Profile

Once the form link goes live, you'll answer 12 questions about your vibe, personality, what you're looking for, and how you move through a room. Takes 5 minutes. No right answers.

02

Get Your Ticket

Velvet Match is a ticketed event — it's not free, and that's the point. The fee keeps the guestlist serious and the venue premium. Confirmed guests only.

03

Show Up & Enjoy the Night

Come dressed. Come curious. Come alone or with one friend. Mix, mingle, eat, drink. The evening is designed so conversations flow naturally. Nobody wears a name tag.

04

The Midnight Match Reveal

When the event starts, a countdown begins behind the scenes. When it hits zero — every guest gets a notification with their top match. Name, a compatibility note, and their contact. The rest is up to the two of you.

The Experience

What to expect on the night.

The Venue

A carefully chosen space — think warm lighting, good music, comfortable seating, and a bar that knows what it's doing. Details drop when you're registered.

Dress Code

Smart casual to elegant. Come looking like someone worth meeting. No athleisure. No hoodies. The vibe is intentional.

The Algorithm

Your 12 answers get cross-matched with every other guest. We weight questions differently — energy, ambition, connection style, pace. The output is your single best match in the room.

Match Notification

Once the event begins, a countdown runs in the background. At the reveal moment, you'll get a notification — your match, their name, and why we think it works. No awkward announcements. Just a quiet buzz in your pocket.

Not Free — By Design

Tickets cost money. That's intentional. People who show up having paid are invested, curious, and present. We'd rather 40 serious guests than 200 people who wandered in.

Couples & Invited Singles Only

This event is open to couples exploring connection and individually invited singles. You can only join via the waitlist — every guest has been through the profile form.
